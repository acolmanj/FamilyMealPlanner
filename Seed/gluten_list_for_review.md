Please review it once against Coeliac Society of Ireland guidance and edit it before the agent runs for real. Whatever you confirm here becomes the rule set for the gluten gate in the app.

## Handling rules

- Where a Tier A or B meal appears in the Menu Semanal (for example pasta, fish fingers, pizza, pancakes, nuggets, tostis, ramen, gnocchi), keep the meal idea and swap to a GF version, and write the swap next to it on the page.
- Roast gravy, soups and stews: use cornflour or a GF-labelled stock, never plain flour.
- Imported recipes: screen every ingredient line and show the result as Safe, Needs substitution or Blocked. Blocked recipes are not used.
- Past purchases are not proof of safety. Do not reuse Weetabix, Corn Flakes, granola, pancakes, beer or chocolate digestives as defaults unless they carry a GF label.
- Dried lentils, beans and rice: buy labelled GF if the pack is not clearly labelled.
- Cross-contact: do not add advice about toasters, pasta water or chopping boards unless the person asks.
